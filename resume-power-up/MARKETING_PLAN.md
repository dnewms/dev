# Marketing Plan - ResumePowerUp

#### Week -3: Content Creation
**Goals**: Build content library, engage community

**Tasks**:

**Goals**: Validate product, gather testimonials

## Marketing Channels Breakdown

### Organic Channels (No Cost)

**1. SEO/Blog** (Effort: High, Timeline: 3-6 months)
- 50+ blog posts in first 90 days
- Target job search keywords
- Build authority in career space

**2. Social Media** (Effort: Medium, Timeline: Immediate)
- Twitter: Daily engagement
- LinkedIn: 3x/week posts
- Instagram: 5x/week posts
- TikTok: 3-5x/week videos

**3. Reddit** (Effort: Medium, Timeline: Immediate)
- Provide value in r/resumes, r/jobs
- Host AMAs
- Share success stories (not spam)

**4. Product Hunt** (Effort: Low, Timeline: Launch day)
- One-time boost
- Potential for viral growth

**5. Community Building** (Effort: High, Timeline: 1-3 months)
- Discord/Slack
- LinkedIn group
- Email newsletter

### Paid Channels (Budget: $3,000/month)

**1. Google Ads** ($1,200/month)
- Target intent keywords
- "AI resume writer"
- "Resume enhancement service"
- Expected CPA: $15-25

**2. Facebook/Instagram Ads** ($1,200/month)
- Job seekers 22-35
- Career changers
- Recent graduates
- Expected CPA: $10-20

**3. LinkedIn Ads** ($600/month)
- Professional audience
- Higher CPA but better quality
- Expected CPA: $30-40

### Partnership Channels (Revenue Share)

**1. Affiliates** (20% commission)
- Career coaches
- Resume writers
- Job search bloggers
- YouTube creators

**2. B2B Partnerships**
- Universities: Educational discount
- Job boards: Integration
- Recruiting agencies: White-label

## Crisis Management

### If Growth is Slow

**Week 1-2**: Double down on Product Hunt
**Week 3-4**: Increase content output (10 posts/week)
**Week 5-8**: Paid ads ($5,000 test)
**Week 9-12**: Partnerships & affiliates

### If Growth is Fast

**Scale**:
- Hire support person
- Increase ad spend
- Accelerate content production
- Build referral program
